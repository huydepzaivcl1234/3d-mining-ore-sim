HUD FADE IN PLACE — Unity 6

1. Close Unity, extract this ZIP into the ROOT folder of your Unity project, and allow replacement of the three listed scripts.
2. Open Unity. The existing scene and prefab references remain intact; you do not need to run a layout tool.
3. In Play Mode, open and close Settings, Upgrade, Rebirth, Inventory, Shop and Quest, and start a game from the Main Menu. Check that each HUD item stays at its authored scene position, fades smoothly, and cannot receive clicks while hidden.
4. Adjust Panel Transition Duration and Modal Backdrop Fade Duration in Assets/GameData/UI/MiningUiData.asset. HUD startup reveal uses the existing MiningHudFlyIn Duration and Delay.

CanvasGroup rule: alpha controls visibility; interactable and blocksRaycasts control clicks. During a fade the HUD is not clickable. After a complete fade-in it becomes clickable. No scene (.unity) file, commit or push is included. The old slide fields remain serialized for safe scene compatibility but no longer move the HUD.
