# Camera-led Shift Lock

- Left Shift toggles lock; mouse look works without holding RMB. Camera yaw stays independent of enemy/player facing, character turns smoothly toward the camera's horizontal forward direction, WASD remains camera-relative so sideways/backward movement is possible. E draw/sheath and left-click attacks remain unchanged.
- Sprint keyboard binding moves from Left Shift to Left Ctrl to avoid one press both sprinting and toggling lock. Gamepad sprint remains unchanged. The Shift Lock InputAction is editable on the existing MiningOrbitCamera component.
- No second camera or Animator/controller replacement. Existing collision, FOV, framing, zoom, VFX and attack damage paths remain intact. Combat suppresses automatic behind-character recentering. Outside combat/lock ordinary behind-player behavior remains.
- Lock disables enemy-driven character auto-turn. Unlocked soft aim acquires one in-range target per strike and turns only for Soft Aim Duration, bounded by Aim Turn Speed, rather than continuously switching to the nearest monster. Legacy F action is retained serialized but no longer enabled or used.
- Cursor ownership is restored on unlock/disable and temporarily released during modal UI, pause, focus loss and cinematics. Death clears lock. Shift Facing Smooth Seconds and Maximum Speed are editable; no camera snap is introduced when toggling.
- Scene authoring is untouched and SampleScene is excluded from delivery. No player save data is changed by this feature.
- MiningOrbitCamera runs before ordinary gameplay scripts so lock input owns ExternalFacing before ThirdPersonController moves; its LateUpdate positions the camera before applying camera-led character yaw. PlayerCombatInput never writes camera yaw.
- Validation: Unity Editor imported/compiled with zero errors. Play Mode on the current scene verified zero camera-yaw drift after turning the combat player 160 degrees, lock disables soft aim, player-facing error smoothly reduced from 160 to 12.53 degrees over 20 simulated camera updates, modal suspend/resume and unlock, and the active PlayerInput Sprint binding resolves to Left Ctrl. Existing unrelated Missing Script warning remains. Physical mouse/key feel and motion sickness require user playtesting; no claim that discomfort can be completely prevented.
