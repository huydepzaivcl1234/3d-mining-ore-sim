# Monster attack volumes

- Shared MushroomMonster exposes Hit Shape: Sweep or Area. Mushroom uses existing range/arc; Golem uses Area Radius and Area Forward Offset. Boss scale affects radius and offsets consistently. Selected gizmos draw the actual sector/circle and height limit, replacing the inaccurate red sphere.
- Hits occur once at Hit Moment in the selected attack clip. All living miners plus the player inside the shape can be struck, with collision closest-point and obstruction checks. Existing life-steal/burn, damage and miner warning behavior are retained. Attack range still determines AI approach distance, not area radius.
- Area center and facing lock at attack start. A runtime ground disk appears in Play Mode; red fills outward using attack normalized time / Hit Moment, including state/Animator speed changes and pause. No separate fixed-delay damage timer. Leaving the disk before contact avoids damage. Death, interruption, disable or expiry hides it.
- Configure fields on each game prefab; Warning Shader is explicitly assigned for build inclusion. Ground disks are reused across attacks, destroyed with their owner, never parented to the moving model. SampleScene is not included in delivery.
- Validation: Unity Editor compiled without errors. Both prefabs passed inside/behind/outside geometry probes. Play Mode verified half-windup gives 0.5 fill, fixed world-space center, contact damage once only, hidden marker after contact, and shader diagnostics with no errors. Positioned screenshot verified circular outline and outward red fill beneath Golem's windup pose. Natural combat balance and uneven terrain are not exhaustively tested.
