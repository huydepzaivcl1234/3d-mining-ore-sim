UPGRADE COST + HOVER/F INTERACTION

1. Giai nen goi nay vao thu muc goc project "3d mining sim".
2. Mo Unity va cho compile xong.
3. Mo SampleScene.
4. Chon menu:
   Mining Simulator > Setup > Create or Update Interaction System
5. Tool se them duy nhat:
   - Mining Interaction System trong UI Systems.
   - Interaction Hover Prompt trong Mining HUD Canvas.
   - Assets/GameData/Interaction/MiningInteractionData.asset.
6. Save Scene sau khi kiem tra vi tri/giao dien prompt.

PORTAL
- Re chuot vao portal: hien [F] INTERACT / [F] TUONG TAC.
- Bam F: mo panel Under Maintenance.
- Bam chuot vao portal khong con mo panel.
- Chay lai menu Create Portal Gate van tai su dung portal cu va khong doi vi tri.

NPC HOI THOAI SAU NAY
- Gan MiningInteractionTarget vao NPC co Collider.
- Dien English Label va Vietnamese Label.
- Noi su kien On Interact vao ham mo panel hoi thoai.
- Co the dung On Focus Entered/Exited cho Outline hoac hieu ung hover.

GIA NANG CAP
- Cong thuc moi: Starting Cost x Cost Growth Multiplier ^ Current Stacks.
- Cost Growth Multiplier nam trong tung muc cua MiningUpgradeData.
- Mac dinh moi la 1.20; NPC Capacity tao moi mac dinh 1.75.
- Gia tri costIncreasePerPurchase cu duoc giu an de khong mat du lieu, nhung khong con duoc dung.
- Goi nay KHONG chua MiningUpgradeData.asset, vi vay khong ghi de cac gia tri ban da chinh.

NGON NGU
- Prompt va ten Portal tu dong doi English/Vietnamese theo MiningLocalization.

Goi nay khong chua Scene, prefab, icon hoac GameData asset va khong commit/push.
