# Immediate NPC engagement and Golem attack pair

- Removed the old miner warning delay and overhead threat countdown creation/update code. Monsters begin their attack animation as soon as a valid target is in Attack Range and cooldown is ready. Damage still waits for animation contact, not instantly upon entering range. Existing miner death, mining and rewards remain unchanged. Ground area warnings remain for both Golem attacks.
- Golem alternates Hammer then Hit for subsequent eligible attacks, not an unconditional queued second hit against a missing/out-of-range target. Mushroom keeps Headbutt with no second state. Both Golem states already exist in the editable GolemCombat controller.
- Separate contact frames/radii/offsets per attack: Hammer normalized 0.5 with sampled average-hand XZ (-0.02, 1.61); Hit normalized 0.52 with sampled right-hand XZ (0.30, 1.71). These local offsets rotate with the monster and scale with bosses. Inspector tuning remains editable, source pack clips unchanged.
- Monster rotates toward its current miner/player target during windup at Attack Turn Speed. Tracking End Fraction defaults 0.85 of the contact windup, then the attack area locks to preserve last-moment dodgeability. Set 1 to track until contact, 0 to lock at start. Ground marker moves with the projected contact center during tracking and uses the same center/radius as damage and gizmos. It does not follow the animated hand backward during the windup.
- Animation Blend Seconds and Chase Turn Speed are configurable, retaining previous defaults. No camera control is touched.
- This update supersedes the old two-second miner warning and attack-start-only direction locking described in MonsterHitVolumes.md and GolemBossEncounters.md.
- Validation: Unity compilation and Console have no errors. Isolated Play Mode probes verified Hammer -> Hit -> Hammer, identical damage totals after a repeated update (one contact per attack), turning 8.29 degrees toward a circling target during one test frame, and immediate Hammer against an actively mining NPC. Sampled hand-to-area horizontal errors were 0.0036m for Hammer and 0.0008m for Hit at contact. Temporary test actors were removed and Play Mode stopped. Existing unrelated Missing Script warning remains. Full natural encounter/balance testing was not performed.
